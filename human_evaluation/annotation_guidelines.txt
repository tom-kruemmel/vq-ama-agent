HUMAN EVALUATION – ANNOTATION GUIDELINES
=========================================

Thank you for participating in this evaluation. You will assess answers
generated by an AI-powered Q&A system for virtualQ.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE
----------------
A spreadsheet (CSV) with 45 rows. Each row contains:
  • annotation_id  – unique row identifier
  • question       – the user's question
  • generated_answer – the system's response

YOUR TASK
---------
For each row, fill in the following columns based on your domain expertise:

1. correctness_1to5
   How factually accurate is the answer?
     1 = Completely wrong or misleading
     2 = Mostly incorrect, with only minor correct elements
     3 = Partially correct, but contains significant errors or gaps
     4 = Mostly correct, with only minor inaccuracies
     5 = Fully correct

2. completeness_1to5
   Does the answer cover all relevant aspects of the question?
     1 = Addresses almost none of the relevant points
     2 = Covers some points but misses major aspects
     3 = Covers the main point but lacks important details
     4 = Covers most relevant aspects with minor omissions
     5 = Fully complete, nothing important is missing

3. usefulness_1to5
   Would this answer actually help someone in a professional context?
     1 = Not useful at all
     2 = Marginally useful
     3 = Somewhat useful but needs significant improvement
     4 = Useful, ready for most practical purposes
     5 = Very useful, clear and actionable

4. notes (optional)
   Any additional comments, e.g. what specifically is wrong or missing.

IMPORTANT NOTES
---------------
• Rate each answer independently. Some questions may appear more than once
  with different answers — this is intentional. Judge each answer on its own.
• Use your domain knowledge. Do not look up answers or consult colleagues
  during annotation. We want your individual expert judgement.
• There are no trick questions. Give honest ratings.
• If you are unsure about a topic, rate to the best of your knowledge and
  add a note.

Thank you!
